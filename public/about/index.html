About Me

Academic background, research motivation, and focus areas.

Academic Profile

I am a Data Science researcher with a solid foundation in Computer Science and Engineering. My academic journey centers on developing machine learning and artificial intelligence solutions tailored to real-world impact, particularly within healthcare analytics and predictive modeling.

I hold a Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science and Engineering with a major in Data Science from Daffodil International University. I earned my Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Computer Science and Engineering from Daffodil International University with a GPA of 3.54.

To further deepen my specialized capabilities, I completed the Government Machine Learning Course under the EDGE initiative, focusing on practical machine learning pipelines, dataset preprocessing, and model deployment strategies.

Research Vision

My research centers on creating interpretable, reliable, and privacy-conscious machine learning models. I am deeply interested in:

  • Healthcare Predictive Analytics: Designing decision-support frameworks to assist early screening and risk stratification.
  • Explainable AI (XAI): Ensuring complex ensemble models remain transparent and understandable for field practitioners.
  • Mental Health Modeling: Applying supervised machine learning and ensemble techniques to clinical data for screening postpartum health conditions.

I aim to continue my academic path through rigorous research, contributing open methodologies and novel insights to the academic community.
